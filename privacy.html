Video Dolz — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Video Dolz is a personal desktop application used by its owner to create, schedule and publish videos to their own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook). It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID) to identify the connected account.
  • Account and video statistics (followers, likes, views, comments, shares) to show performance analytics.
  • The list of the account's own videos, to match published posts with analytics.
  • Permission to upload videos to the account's TikTok inbox / platform drafts or schedule.

How data is stored

All data is stored locally on the owner's own computer and paired phone. Access tokens are encrypted. No data is sent to any server operated by Video Dolz; the app only communicates directly with the official platform APIs.

Sharing

Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the platform's own settings (e.g. TikTok → Settings → Security → Apps and services).
